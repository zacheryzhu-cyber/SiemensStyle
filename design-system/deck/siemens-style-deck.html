Usage

When to use what

Data chart colours

  • Complex visualisations in software products
  • Identifiers, avatars and colour-coded elements
  • Follow the numbered sequence, dark or light values
  • Prefer yellow over purple when both are needed

Not for marketing

  • Do not use data chart colours in online marketing
  • Use the separate marketing colour set instead
  • No 3D effects, no extra fonts
  • Never use colour alone to carry meaning

Building HTML slides

Rules for every HTML deck

Must

No scroll bar

Slides fit the viewport. The page never shows a scroll bar on the right. Size type and spacing with vw, vh and clamp(), and split a slide when it overflows.

html,body{overflow:hidden}
Motion

Hover feedback

Cards lift 3 to 4 px, glow in mint and take the accent fill. Keep each change under 200 ms.

transform:translateY(-4px)
Motion

Staggered entrance

Elements rise in 70 ms apart when a slide opens. One gentle motion, nothing looping.

animation-delay:calc(var(--i)*70ms)
